# Hotel-Data-Analysis-Project

Project Overview


The Hotel Data Analysis Project is a data analytics project developed using Google Sheets to analyze hotel bookings, cancellations, revenue, customer types, market segments, and booking trends. The project involves data cleaning, transformation, analysis, and visualization to identify important patterns and business insights from hotel booking data.

The analysis covers Resort Hotel and City Hotel bookings and examines key metrics such as total bookings, cancelled and non-cancelled bookings, cancellation percentage, yearly revenue, market segment performance, and customer types. Pivot tables, formulas, charts, and interactive dashboards were used to present the findings in a clear and understandable format.

The project demonstrates practical skills in data cleaning, data analysis, KPI calculation, pivot tables, data visualization, and business insight generation using Google Sheets.

In this  project  there  are  two  hotels City hotel  and  Resort Hotel

The analysis evaluates the overall booking volume and cancellation behavior of the hotel. Total Bookings represents all recorded reservations, while Cancelled Bookings identifies reservations that were cancelled. Not Cancelled Bookings represents reservations that remained active.

The Cancellation Percentage is calculated by dividing cancelled bookings by total bookings and multiplying by 100. Similarly, the Not Cancellation Percentage is calculated by dividing non-cancelled bookings by total bookings and multiplying by 100.


Total Bookings = Cancelled Bookings + Not Cancelled Bookings

Cancellation % =
(Cancelled Bookings / Total Bookings) × 100

Not Cancellation % =
(Not Cancelled Bookings / Total Bookings) × 100

The Hotel Booking Data Analysis provides a comprehensive overview of booking patterns and customer behavior for Resort Hotel and City Hotel. The analysis covers total bookings, ADR, lead time, room nights, length of stay, cancellation rates, market segments, and customer types.

The results show that City Hotel has a higher number of total bookings and ADR, while Resort Hotel records more total room nights and a longer average length of stay. City Hotel also has a higher reported cancellation rate. The market segment and customer type analysis shows differences in booking sources and customer composition between the two hotels, with notable variation in Group, Online TA, Transient, and Transient-Party bookings.

Overall, the project demonstrates how Google Sheets and pivot-table-based analysis can be used to organize hotel booking data, calculate important KPIs, compare hotel performance, and identify meaningful patterns in customer and booking behavior.
